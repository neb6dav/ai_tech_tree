# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the AI Research Tech Tree a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

The project deals with disputed histories, competing priority claims, and uncertain evidence. Disagreement is expected; personal attacks and misrepresentation are not.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a healthy community include:

- focusing criticism on claims, sources, methods, and representations rather than people;
- clearly separating evidence, interpretation, and speculation;
- acknowledging uncertainty, conflicts of interest, and corrections;
- giving specific, actionable, and proportionate review feedback;
- respecting privacy, attribution, and intellectual-property rights;
- accepting responsibility for mistakes and repairing harm where possible; and
- showing patience toward contributors with different expertise or communication styles.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, or sexualized attention;
- insults, trolling, inflammatory personal commentary, or sustained disruption;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- fabricating sources, impersonating contributors, or knowingly misrepresenting evidence;
- pressuring contributors to reveal protected or private information;
- abusing issue or review processes to target a person; and
- other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional research community.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, release channels, and public spaces when someone is representing this project or its community.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate them. Proportionate responses may include a private warning, temporary participation limits, removal of contributions, or a permanent ban.

Report conduct concerns privately to [@neb6dav](https://github.com/neb6dav) using a contact method published on that profile. Do not put sensitive report details in a public issue. If no private method is available, open a neutral issue requesting a private contact channel without describing the incident publicly.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as practical. The maintainer will respect the reporter's privacy and safety, subject to legal obligations and the need to investigate.
