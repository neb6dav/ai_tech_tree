Research questions and open direction

Questions

  • Can active-inference objectives produce compute-matched agents that equal strong model-based reinforcement-learning baselines on planning, adaptation, robustness, and efficiency?

Open direction

Can active-inference objectives produce compute-matched agents that equal strong model-based reinforcement-learning baselines on planning, adaptation, robustness, and efficiency?

Closure criteria

Under identical observations, model capacity, environment steps, and tuning budgets, an active-inference agent matches or exceeds strong model-based baselines across several environment families, with objective ablations identifying where expected-free-energy terms add value.

Claim state: Editorial hypothesis · High confidence

Partial results

  • Deep active inference with Monte Carlo tree search plans in dSprites and Animal-AI environments.
  • Discrete active-inference agents and the maintained pymdp library establish reproducible small-scale feasibility.

Counterexamples and limits

  • Fountas et al. demonstrate a deep active-inference MCTS agent in continuous-state simulations.
  • Sajid et al. compare active inference with reinforcement learning in discrete Gym-style settings.
  • pymdp provides a maintained implementation for discrete Markov decision processes.

Crowdedness: Low to medium; active communities exist, but large compute-matched benchmark studies are uncommon.

Tractability: High for controlled comparisons and medium for broad generalization or robotics.

Suggested starting points

  • Individual: Implement a pymdp or neural active-inference agent on MiniGrid and compare it with PPO and a model-based baseline under fixed environment-step and tuning budgets.
  • Small lab: Run preregistered objective ablations across MiniGrid, Procgen, and selected DeepMind Control Suite tasks, measuring adaptation after dynamics and reward shifts.
  • Well-funded lab: Build a distributed active-inference agent and conduct a compute-matched simulation-to-robot evaluation with robustness, calibration, and wall-clock efficiency measurements.
