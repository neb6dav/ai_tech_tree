Research questions and open direction

Questions

  • Can local learning approach backpropagation at useful scale while delivering a measured hardware, memory, communication, or energy advantage?

Open direction

Can local learning approach backpropagation at useful scale while delivering a measured hardware, memory, communication, or energy advantage?

Closure criteria

On a useful-scale task and matched architecture, a local rule closes most of the accuracy and robustness gap to backpropagation while demonstrating reproducible end-to-end energy, memory, communication, or latency gains that include host and data-movement overhead.

Claim state: Editorial hypothesis · High confidence

Partial results

  • Forward-Forward replaces forward and backward passes with positive and negative forward passes but was introduced on small problems.
  • CLAPP-like local self-supervised learning matches comparable global objectives on CIFAR-10, STL-10, and Tiny ImageNet.
  • Predictive coding can approximate backpropagation on arbitrary computation graphs in theory and small implementations.

Counterexamples and limits

  • Local CLAPP variants match comparable global self-supervised objectives on several small vision datasets.
  • Predictive-coding updates can approximate automatic differentiation on arbitrary computation graphs.
  • Neuromorphic systems show task-specific energy advantages, although often without matched learning rules and end-to-end accounting.

Novelty review

Status: Targeted search. Checked: 2026-08-04. arXiv and official repositories for Forward-Forward, predictive coding, local self-supervised learning, neuromorphic learning, and hardware-energy measurement. Partial algorithmic and hardware results exist, but the search did not find a useful-scale system showing both backpropagation-level performance and end-to-end systems advantage.

Tractability: High for small-scale algorithmic work and low for credible useful-scale systems claims.

Suggested starting points

  • Individual: Compare backpropagation, CLAPP, predictive coding, and Forward-Forward on identical Tiny ImageNet networks while measuring stored activations, communication, memory, and accuracy.
  • Small lab: Scale the strongest local self-supervised rule to ImageNet-100 or ImageNet-1k with matched ResNet or ViT baselines and NeuroBench-style operation metrics.
  • Well-funded lab: Implement the same local rule on neuromorphic or analog hardware and publish wall-plug energy, latency, robustness, and host-overhead comparisons against a conventional backpropagation system.
