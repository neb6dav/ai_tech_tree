Open direction

When do explicit intervention and counterfactual structures improve learned-world planning under distribution shift relative to equally capable noncausal world models?

Closure criteria

On held-out interventions and mechanism changes, a causal world model improves planning success, calibration, and sample efficiency over matched latent-dynamics and invariant-learning baselines, with gains surviving misspecified graphs and latent confounding.

Claim state: Editorial hypothesis · Medium confidence

Partial results

  • Causal World Models use unsupervised deconfounding and report improvements in reinforcement-learning sample complexity and counterfactual physical reasoning.
  • CausalVerse provides high-fidelity configurable simulations with known causal structure and intervention histories.
  • Existing causal-control systems remain mainly simulated or sensitive to structural assumptions.

Counterexamples and limits

  • Causal World Models explicitly combine learned dynamics, latent deconfounding, counterfactual prediction, and reinforcement learning.
  • Explainable reinforcement learning via causal world models combines causal structure with control and explanation.
  • Interventional causal-representation methods learn structure from intervention families in controlled settings.
