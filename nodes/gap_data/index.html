Summary

Research direction: sustain capability improvement under a constrained or synthetic-only data supply while preventing collapse, contamination and diversity loss. Collapse is not inevitable when real-data anchors or external diversity controls remain; the stricter synthetic-only capability ratchet remains unresolved.

Research questions and open direction

Questions

  • Can repeated generations of synthetic or self-generated experience preserve or improve held-out capability and diversity without fresh human data, contamination, or a hidden stronger teacher?

Open direction

Can repeated generations of synthetic or self-generated experience preserve or improve held-out capability and diversity without fresh human data, contamination, or a hidden stronger teacher?

Closure criteria

Run multiple recursive generations with immutable held-out human evaluations and provenance; measure average quality, tail coverage, diversity, calibration, memorization, and contamination each round; forbid fresh human data and undisclosed stronger-teacher labels after initialization; compare replacement, accumulation, curation, multi-teacher, and self-training regimes; and reproduce independently.

Claim state: Editorial hypothesis · Medium confidence

Partial results

  • Recursive replacement with generated data can erase distribution tails and cause model collapse. Accumulating synthetic data with the original real distribution can avoid collapse in several modalities, and diverse or pluralistic curation can stabilize bounded regimes. These are genuine counterexamples to inevitability, but they retain real-data anchors, external rewards, multiple teachers, or bounded tasks and do not yet show a sustained synthetic-only capability ratchet.

Counterexamples and limits

  • The Curse of Recursion demonstrates progressive loss of distribution tails when generated data replace real data across language and generative-model settings.
  • Is Model Collapse Inevitable? shows that accumulating synthetic data alongside the original real data avoids collapse in language, molecular, and image experiments; this does not satisfy a no-fresh-anchor synthetic-only loop.
  • Recent curated, diverse-teacher, and pluralistic-reward systems can delay or prevent collapse in bounded settings, but depend on external selection signals, anchored data, or stronger and diverse teachers.

Novelty review

Status: Targeted search. Checked: 2026-08-04. Targeted arXiv searches for model collapse, recursive synthetic-data training, synthetic-data accumulation, curated synthetic data, pluralistic rewards, multi-teacher self-training, contamination, experience generation, and 2025-2026 recursive-loop studies; reference chaining from the two representative papers. Medium confidence that the synthetic-only, repeated capability-improvement gap remains open. Real-data accumulation or externally preserved diversity can prevent collapse in bounded settings; the unresolved target therefore excludes fresh anchors and hidden stronger teachers. No priority claim is made.

Estimated effort: Individual: One to four GPUs for 100M-1B models over 5-20 rounds. · SmallLab: Sixteen to sixty-four GPUs for 1B-8B models, 10-30 rounds, and continuous provenance and contamination analysis. · WellFundedLab: Frontier-scale training allocation for 70B or multimodal recursive loops, long-term dataset storage, and an independent data-audit team.

Crowdedness: High; synthetic data, self-training, and model collapse are active topics, but deeply controlled multi-generation studies remain expensive and less common.

Tractability: High for causal small-model studies, medium for 1B-8B replication, and low for frontier-scale conclusions.

Suggested starting points

  • Individual: Train a 100M-1B model for 10-20 recursive rounds under replacement, accumulation, and diversity-curation conditions using one cryptographically frozen human holdout and complete data lineage.
  • Small lab: Run a 1B-8B text or multimodal study for 10-30 rounds, auditing contamination, rare-skill retention, diversity, calibration, and teacher dependence with external evaluators fixed before training.
  • Well-funded lab: Run a 70B-scale, 30-generation multi-teacher experiment with no fresh human data after initialization, immutable external evaluation, explicit teacher-capability accounting, and an independent provenance and contamination audit.
