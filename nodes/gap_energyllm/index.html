Summary

Research direction: compare energy-based, diffusion-language-model and JEPA-style non-autoregressive systems with strong autoregressive baselines at equal data, parameter, inference-compute and latency budgets. Capable 8B-14B non-autoregressive systems already exist; robust parity across broad language tasks, efficiency and controllability remains unresolved.

Research questions and open direction

Questions

  • Can a genuinely energy-based or non-autoregressive language objective match the strongest autoregressive models under equal data, parameters, training compute, and serving budgets across capability, efficiency, and controllability?

Open direction

Can a genuinely energy-based or non-autoregressive language objective match the strongest autoregressive models under equal data, parameters, training compute, and serving budgets across capability, efficiency, and controllability?

Closure criteria

Run a preregistered scaling study with shared data, tokenizer, parameter and FLOP budgets, followed by matched-quality serving comparisons across capability, latency, throughput, controllability, and calibration.

Claim state: Editorial hypothesis · Medium confidence

Partial results

  • Energy-Based Transformers learn compatibility energies and perform inference through energy minimization.
  • iLLaDA is an 8B masked-diffusion language model trained on 12 trillion tokens and is competitive with Qwen2.5-7B on several benchmarks.
  • LLaDA, SEDD, and Nemotron-Labs-Diffusion demonstrate scalable non-autoregressive or hybrid alternatives.

Counterexamples and limits

  • Energy-Based Transformers directly instantiate an energy-based language architecture.
  • LLaDA and iLLaDA show that masked diffusion can scale to 8B parameters and trillions of tokens.
  • Nemotron-Labs-Diffusion releases 3B, 8B, and 14B hybrid autoregressive-diffusion models.
  • SEDD reports competitive discrete-diffusion language modeling at smaller scale.

Novelty review

Status: Targeted search. Checked: 2026-08-04. arXiv and official repositories for energy-based transformers, discrete diffusion, masked language diffusion, joint-embedding prediction, and large non-autoregressive language models. Large partial systems exist, so the defensible gap is exact equal-budget broad parity rather than absence of scale; diffusion models are adjacent and should not all be labeled energy-based.

Suggested starting points

  • Individual: Benchmark released autoregressive, diffusion, and energy-based checkpoints under matched answer quality, reporting latency, function evaluations, memory, and controllable infilling.
  • Small lab: Train matched 300M-1B autoregressive, masked-diffusion, and energy-based models on one frozen corpus and publish complete learning and serving curves.
  • Well-funded lab: Run a preregistered 7B-14B matched-data scaling trial including instruction tuning, code, reasoning, long-form generation, and production-serving cost.
