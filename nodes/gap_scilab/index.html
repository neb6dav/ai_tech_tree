Research questions and open direction

Questions

  • Can one autonomous scientific system transfer across instruments and scientific domains while keeping experiments safe, uncertainty calibrated, provenance complete, and results independently reproducible?
  • Which parts of scientific judgment transfer across domains, and how should autonomous labs document uncertainty and failed experiments?

Open direction

Can one autonomous scientific system transfer across instruments and scientific domains while keeping experiments safe, uncertainty calibrated, provenance complete, and results independently reproducible?

Closure criteria

Pre-register transfer from at least one training domain or instrument to two materially different targets; require calibrated abstention and human safety gates; preserve machine-readable lineage from hypothesis through raw data and analysis; compare against domain-specific automation and human teams; and have an independent lab reproduce a claimed result from the released record.

Claim state: Editorial hypothesis · High confidence

Partial results

  • A 2026 autonomous laboratory agent operates scanning probe microscopy through a domain-specific model and deterministic modular interface. AI Scientist-v2 automates computational machine-learning research across several subdomains, and earlier Coscientist work demonstrated bounded chemistry workflows. These systems establish real partial autonomy, but none closes cross-instrument, cross-domain transfer with independent physical reproduction and calibrated uncertainty.

Counterexamples and limits

  • Autonomous Laboratory Agent controls real-time atomic-resolution scanning probe microscopy using a customized domain model, deterministic validation, and modular orchestration; it remains a single instrument family and domain.
  • AI Scientist-v2 performs template-free computational ML research and produced workshop-level manuscripts, including one accepted at a workshop with a high acceptance rate; it does not demonstrate transfer across physical instruments or independent experimental reproduction.
  • Coscientist and other self-driving-lab systems demonstrate chemistry and materials workflows, while DiscoveryBench and ScienceAgentBench show that broad data-science agents still solve only a minority of realistic tasks.

Novelty review

Status: Targeted search. Checked: 2026-08-04. Targeted arXiv searches for autonomous laboratories, self-driving labs, AI scientists, laboratory agents, cross-domain scientific agents, instrument control, reproducibility, provenance, Coscientist, AI Scientist v2, and 2025-2026 systems and benchmarks; reference chaining from representative systems and benchmark papers. High confidence that transferable cross-instrument, cross-domain autonomy with independent physical replication remains open. Domain-specific and computational systems are already substantial, so the gap must not be described as absence of autonomous science. No priority claim is made.

Estimated effort: Individual: One to four GPUs and data-only or simulated laboratories; no unsupervised wet-lab or physical actuation. · SmallLab: Four to sixteen GPUs, two API-controlled instruments or one instrument plus simulator, technician time, metrology, and safety review. · WellFundedLab: Thirty-two or more GPUs, two domains and multiple workcells, robotic sample handling, instrument engineers, metrology, research-integrity controls, and an independent replication site.

Crowdedness: High for computational science agents, medium for domain-specific self-driving labs, and low for the same system transferring across physical domains and sites.

Tractability: High for data-only provenance studies, medium for a single-instrument system, and low for cross-domain transfer with independent physical reproduction.

Suggested starting points

  • Individual: Build a provenance-first agent for DiscoveryBench and ScienceAgentBench that must produce an executable rerun bundle, calibrated confidence, and an explicit abstention whenever evidence or permissions are missing.
  • Small lab: Connect the same policy layer to two instruments, or one instrument plus a high-fidelity simulator, preregister transfer tasks, retain human safety approval, and have a second operator independently repeat the result.
  • Well-funded lab: Run a two-domain, two-site autonomous-science challenge with different instruments, robotic sample handling, blind replication, machine-readable lineage, uncertainty calibration, and independent safety and research-integrity oversight.
